# Branding assets

The project name, logo and artwork are reserved separately from the MIT-licensed
code. See [license scope and branding](../NOTICE.md) before reusing these assets.

The social preview follows the application's dark theme: charcoal background
(`#121318`), panel surface (`#17181e`), pale text (`#e4e9f4`), muted text
(`#96a2b9`), blue accent (`#9bbfea`) and borders (`#2a3448`).

The 2400 × 1260 artwork is maintained in these synchronized assets:

- [Root JPEG](../og.jpg)
- [Frontend JPEG](../frontend/public/assets/og.jpg)
- [Frontend PNG](../frontend/public/assets/social-preview.png)

The brand and summation mark sit on the left, with a mathematical formula panel
on the right. Keep exports synchronized when changing the artwork.
The favicon uses a summation symbol and the console banner uses the PermaFormula name.

Development social-card URLs use `PERMAFORMULA_DEV_UI_ORIGIN`; packaged deployments
use the backend's `PERMAFORMULA_HOST`. See the [frontend guide](../frontend/README.md).
